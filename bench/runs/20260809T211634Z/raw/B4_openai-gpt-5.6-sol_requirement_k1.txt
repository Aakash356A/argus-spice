* AD8551 non-inverting amplifier, gain = 100 V/V, single 5 V supply
.include AD8551.cir

VCC VCC 0 5
VREF VREF 0 2.5
VIN IN 0 SINE(2.5 10m 1k) AC 10m

RG NFB VREF 1k
RF OUT NFB 99k
CLOAD OUT 0 10p

XU1 IN NFB VCC 0 OUT AD8551

.tran 0 10m 5m 1u
.ac dec 100 10 100k
.meas AC gain_db FIND db(V(OUT)/V(IN)) AT=1k

.end
