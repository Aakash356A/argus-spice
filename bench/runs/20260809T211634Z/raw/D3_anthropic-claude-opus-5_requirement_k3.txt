* ================================================================
* AD8237 Micropower Zero-Drift Instrumentation Amplifier
* Gain = 10 V/V, single +5 V supply, REF at mid-supply (2.5 V)
* Differential input: 20 mV amplitude sine @ 1 kHz
* ================================================================
* Genuine Analog Devices AD8237 macromodel must be available:
.include AD8237.cir
* (subckt pin order: +IN -IN +VS -VS OUT REF FB)

* ---------------- Supplies ----------------
VCC     VCC   0     DC 5
* Mid-supply reference for single-supply operation
VREF    VREF  0     DC 2.5

* ---------------- Differential signal source ----------------
* 20 mV amplitude (40 mV p-p) differential, 1 kHz, riding on VREF
VIN     IN    VREF  SINE(0 20m 1k)

* ---------------- Instrumentation amplifier ----------------
* Gain = 1 + R2/R1 = 1 + 9k/1k = 10 V/V (20 dB)
XU1     IN    VREF  VCC   0     OUT   VREF  FB   AD8237
R1      FB    VREF  1k
R2      OUT   FB    9k

* ---------------- Load ----------------
RL      OUT   0     100k
CL      OUT   0     100p

* ---------------- Analysis ----------------
.tran 0 5m 0 1u

.meas TRAN vout_pp PP V(OUT)      FROM 1m TO 5m
.meas TRAN vin_pp  PP V(IN,VREF)  FROM 1m TO 5m
.meas TRAN gain_vv PARAM vout_pp/vin_pp
.meas TRAN gain_db PARAM 20*log10(vout_pp/vin_pp)
.meas TRAN vout_dc AVG V(OUT)     FROM 1m TO 5m

.options plotwinsize=0 numdgt=7
.end
