* LTspice netlist for LT6106 High-Side Current Sense Amplifier
* Monitoring 100 mA through a 0.1 ohm shunt on a 12 V rail
* Target VOUT = 1.0 V

* Power Supply (12V rail)
V1 IN 0 12

* Load drawing 100 mA
I1 LOAD 0 100m

* Shunt Resistor (0.1 ohm)
* Voltage drop = 100mA * 0.1ohm = 10mV
Rsense IN LOAD 0.1

* LT6106 Gain Setting Resistors
* The internal amplifier forces the -IN pin to the same voltage as the +IN pin.
* With +IN connected to the supply side and -IN to the load side via Rin, 
* the 10mV sense voltage is impressed across Rin.
* I_Rin = 10mV / 100 ohms = 100 uA
Rin LOAD IN_MINUS 100

* The 100 uA current is mirrored/passed to the OUT pin.
* Vout = I_Rin * Rout = 100 uA * 10k = 1.0 V
Rout OUT 0 10k

* LT6106 Subcircuit Instantiation
* Pin order typically: +IN -IN V+ V- OUT
* +IN is tied to the supply side of the shunt (IN)
* -IN is tied to Rin (IN_MINUS)
* V+ is tied to the 12V supply (IN)
* V- is tied to GND (0)
X1 IN IN_MINUS IN 0 OUT LT6106

* Analysis directive to calculate the DC operating point and allow settling
.tran 1m
