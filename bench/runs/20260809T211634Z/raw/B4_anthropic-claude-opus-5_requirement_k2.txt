* AD8551 Non-Inverting Amplifier, Gain = 100 V/V (40 dB), Single +5 V Supply
* Input: 10 mV amplitude, 1 kHz sine, biased at mid-supply (2.5 V)
* Genuine ADI AD8551 macromodel is instantiated as a subcircuit below.

*----------------------------------------------------------------
* Vendor model (Analog Devices AD8551 SPICE macromodel)
* Pin order: +IN  -IN  V+  V-  OUT
*----------------------------------------------------------------
.include AD8551.cir

*----------------------------------------------------------------
* Supplies and mid-supply reference
*----------------------------------------------------------------
VCC   VCC  0   DC 5
VREF  VREF 0   DC 2.5

*----------------------------------------------------------------
* Signal source: 10 mV amplitude sine at 1 kHz, 2.5 V DC bias
*----------------------------------------------------------------
V1    IN   0   DC 2.5 AC 1 SINE(2.5 10m 1k)

*----------------------------------------------------------------
* Amplifier: non-inverting, gain = 1 + Rf/Rg = 1 + 99k/1k = 100 V/V
* Rg returns to the 2.5 V reference so the output sits at mid-supply
*----------------------------------------------------------------
XU1   IN   INN  VCC  0   OUT  AD8551
Rf    OUT  INN  99k
Rg    INN  VREF 1k
RL    OUT  0    100k
CL    OUT  0    10p

*----------------------------------------------------------------
* Analysis
*----------------------------------------------------------------
.tran 0 5m 0 1u

.meas TRAN vinpp  PP V(IN)  FROM 1m TO 5m
.meas TRAN voutpp PP V(OUT) FROM 1m TO 5m
.meas TRAN gain      PARAM voutpp/vinpp
.meas TRAN gain_db   PARAM 20*log10(voutpp/vinpp)
.meas TRAN vout_dc   AVG V(OUT) FROM 1m TO 5m

.options plotwinsize=0 reltol=1e-4

.end
