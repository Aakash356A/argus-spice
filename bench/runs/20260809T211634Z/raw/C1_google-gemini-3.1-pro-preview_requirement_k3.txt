```spice
* LTC6252 High-Speed Amplifier

* Power Supplies (+/- 5V)
V1 VCC 0 5
V2 VEE 0 -5

* Input Signal: 1 V amplitude sine wave at 10 MHz
V3 IN 0 SINE(0 1 10Meg)

* Feedback network for Non-Inverting Gain = 2 V/V
* Gain = 1 + R2/R1 = 1 + 1k/1k = 2 V/V
R1 M_IN 0 1k
R2 OUT M_IN 1k

* LTC6252 Opamp Subcircuit Instantiation
* Standard LTspice pinout for opamps with shutdown: +IN -IN V+ V- OUT SHDN
* SHDN is tied to VCC to enable the amplifier
X1 IN M_IN VCC VEE OUT VCC LTC6252

* Transient analysis for 2 microseconds (20 cycles at 10 MHz)
* Max timestep set to 1ns for good resolution
.tran 0 2u 0 1n

* Measure peak-to-peak voltages over the second microsecond (to allow for any initial settling)
.meas TRAN out_pp PP V(OUT) FROM 1u TO 2u
.meas TRAN in_pp PP V(IN) FROM 1u TO 2u

* Calculate the gain in dB
.meas TRAN gain_db PARAM 20*log10(out_pp/in_pp)

.end
```
