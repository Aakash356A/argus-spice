* LT3045-1 Ultralow Noise LDO: 5V IN -> 3.3V OUT @ 100mA
* ---------------------------------------------------------------
* Genuine ADI/LTC LT3045-1 macromodel is instantiated as a subcircuit
.include LT3045-1.sub

*** Input source (5 V rail) ***
V1 IN 0 DC 5

*** Input capacitor ***
Cin IN 0 10u

*** LT3045-1 LDO regulator ***
* Node order: IN  OUT  OUTS  SET  ILIM  PG  EN/UV  GND
* OUTS is Kelvin-tied to OUT, ILIM grounded for default (max) current limit,
* EN/UV tied to IN for always-enabled operation.
XU1 IN OUT OUT SET 0 PG IN 0 LT3045-1

*** Output voltage programming: VOUT = 100uA * RSET = 100u * 33k = 3.3 V ***
RSET SET 0 33k

*** Output capacitor (>=10uF required for stability) ***
Cout OUT 0 10u

*** Power-good open-collector pull-up ***
RPG PG OUT 100k

*** Resistive load: 3.3 V / 33 ohm = 100 mA ***
RLOAD OUT 0 33

*** Analysis ***
.tran 0 5m 0 1u
.meas TRAN vout_dc AVG V(OUT) FROM 4m TO 5m
.meas TRAN iload AVG I(RLOAD) FROM 4m TO 5m

.options plotwinsize=0 gmin=1e-12 reltol=1e-4
.end
