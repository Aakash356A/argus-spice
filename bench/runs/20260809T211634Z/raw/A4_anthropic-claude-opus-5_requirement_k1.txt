* =====================================================================
* ADR1399H -- Ultrahigh Precision 7.05 V Heated Buried Zener Reference
* Basic shunt-mode reference: 15 V supply, series bias resistor,
* output bypass capacitor and a light resistive load.
* =====================================================================

* ---- Genuine ADI device model (do not redefine) ----
.include ADR1399H.cir

* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Supply
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
V1 IN 0 DC 15

* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Zener bias resistor:  Iz = (15 V - 7.05 V) / 7.87k ~= 1.01 mA
* (datasheet recommended operating current ~1 mA)
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
R1 IN OUT 7.87k

* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
* ADR1399H  (TO-46 pin order: 1 = HEATER+, 2 = ANODE(-VZ),
*                             3 = CATHODE(+VZ), 4 = HEATER-)
* Heater is powered directly from the 15 V rail.
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
X1 IN 0 OUT 0 ADR1399H

* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Output bypass capacitor and light load (~70 uA)
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
C1 OUT 0 1u
RL OUT 0 100k

* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
* Analysis: run long enough for the internal heater/oven and the
* output network to settle, then average the settled output.
* ---------------------------------------------------------------------
.tran 0 2 1.5 100u

.meas TRAN vout_dc AVG V(OUT) FROM=1.9 TO=2.0
.meas TRAN iz_bias AVG I(R1) FROM=1.9 TO=2.0

.options plotwinsize=0 reltol=1e-4 gmin=1e-13 method=gear

.end
