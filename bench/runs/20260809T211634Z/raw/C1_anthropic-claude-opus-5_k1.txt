* LTC6252 High-Speed Op Amp -- Non-Inverting Gain of 2 (+6.02 dB), +/-5 V Supplies
* Primary input node: IN     Primary output node: OUT

*---------------- Supplies ----------------
VCC VCC 0 DC 5
VEE VEE 0 DC -5

*---------------- Input source: 1 V amplitude, 10 MHz sine ----------------
VIN IN 0 SINE(0 1 10MEG) AC 1

*---------------- Amplifier ----------------
* LTC6252 pin order: +IN  -IN  V+  V-  OUT
XU1 IN FB VCC VEE OUT LTC6252

*---------------- Gain-setting network: Av = 1 + Rf/Rg = 2 ----------------
RF OUT FB 249
RG FB 0 249

*---------------- Supply decoupling and load ----------------
CBP1 VCC 0 100n
CBP2 VEE 0 100n
RL OUT 0 1k
CL OUT 0 2p

*---------------- Model library ----------------
.lib LTC.lib

*---------------- Analysis ----------------
.tran 0 1u 0 100p
* Gain measurement from settled portion of the transient
.meas TRAN vinpp  PP V(IN)  FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN voutpp PP V(OUT) FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN gain   PARAM voutpp/vinpp
.meas TRAN gain_db PARAM 20*log10(voutpp/vinpp)

* Optional small-signal frequency response check
* .ac dec 100 10k 1G

.options plotwinsize=0
.end
