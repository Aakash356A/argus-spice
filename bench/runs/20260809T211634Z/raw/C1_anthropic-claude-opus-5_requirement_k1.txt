* LTC6252 High-Speed Op Amp -- Non-Inverting Gain of 2 (+6.02 dB)
* Supplies: +/-5 V, Input: 1 V amplitude sine at 10 MHz
*
*** Supplies ***
VCC VCC 0 DC 5
VEE VEE 0 DC -5
CB1 VCC 0 1u
CB2 VEE 0 1u
*
*** Input source ***
VIN IN 0 SINE(0 1 10MEG) AC 1
*
*** Amplifier: pins = +IN  -IN  V+  V-  OUT ***
XU1 IN FB VCC VEE OUT LTC6252
*
*** Non-inverting gain network:  Av = 1 + RF/RG = 2 ***
RG FB 0 1k
RF OUT FB 1k
*
*** Load ***
RL OUT 0 1k
CL OUT 0 2p
*
*** Model ***
.include LTC6252.sub
*
*** Analysis ***
.tran 0 1u 0 100p
*
*** Measurements ***
.meas TRAN vomax MAX V(OUT) FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN vomin MIN V(OUT) FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN vinmax MAX V(IN) FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN vinmin MIN V(IN) FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN gain PARAM (vomax-vomin)/(vinmax-vinmin)
.meas TRAN gain_db PARAM 20*log10((vomax-vomin)/(vinmax-vinmin))
*
.options plotwinsize=0 numdgt=7
.end
