```
* LT3045-1 Ultralow Noise LDO: 5V IN -> 3.3V OUT @ 100mA
* -----------------------------------------------------------
* VOUT = ISET(100uA) * RSET  ->  RSET = 33k for 3.3V
* -----------------------------------------------------------

*** Input source ***
VIN IN 0 DC 5

*** Input capacitor ***
CIN IN 0 10u

*** Regulator: pins IN OUT OUTS SET ILIM EN/UV PG GND ***
XU1 IN OUT OUT SET ILIM IN PG 0 LT3045-1

*** Output voltage programming resistor (100uA x 33k = 3.3V) ***
RSET SET 0 33k

*** Current limit pin: left effectively open -> internal max limit ***
RILIM ILIM 0 1G

*** Power good pull-up ***
RPG PG OUT 100k

*** Output capacitor ***
COUT OUT 0 10u

*** 100 mA resistive load (3.3V / 33ohm) ***
RLOAD OUT 0 33

*** Model library ***
.include LT3045-1.lib

*** Analysis ***
.tran 0 5m 0 1u startup
.meas TRAN vout_dc AVG V(OUT) FROM 4m TO 5m
.meas TRAN iload AVG I(RLOAD) FROM 4m TO 5m

.options plotwinsize=0
.end
```
