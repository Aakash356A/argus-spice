* LT6106 High-Side Current Sense Amplifier
* 100 mA load current through a 0.1 ohm shunt on a 12 V rail
* Gain = ROUT/RIN = 10k/100 = 100 V/V
* Vsense = 100 mA * 0.1 ohm = 10 mV  ->  VOUT = 10 mV * 100 = 1.0 V

.lib LTC7.lib

*** Supply / monitored rail ***
V1   IN   0   DC 12

*** Sense resistor (high side) ***
RSENSE IN LOAD 0.1

*** Load: 119.9 ohm in series with 0.1 ohm shunt -> 12 V / 120 ohm = 100 mA ***
RLOAD  LOAD 0  119.9

*** LT6106 current sense amplifier
*  Pin order: 1 = -IN, 2 = V-, 3 = OUT, 4 = V+, 5 = +IN
XU1  INM  0  OUT  IN  IN  LT6106

*** Gain-setting resistors ***
* RIN sets the sense current: I_IN = Vsense/RIN = 10 mV/100 = 100 uA
RIN   LOAD INM  100
* ROUT converts the mirrored current to the output voltage: VOUT = I_IN * ROUT
ROUT  OUT  0    10k
* Optional output noise/bandwidth filter (real design)
COUT  OUT  0    100p

*** Analysis ***
.tran 0 5m 0 1u
.meas TRAN vout_dc AVG V(OUT) FROM=3m TO=5m
.meas TRAN isense AVG I(RSENSE) FROM=3m TO=5m

.end
