AD8551 Non-Inverting Amplifier, Gain = 100 V/V
.lib ADI.lib

VCC VCC 0 5
VREF VREF 0 2.5
VIN IN 0 DC 2.5 AC 10m SINE(2.5 10m 1k)

RG NINV VREF 1k
RF OUT NINV 99k

XU1 IN NINV VCC 0 OUT AD8551

CDEC VCC 0 100n

.ac dec 100 10 100k
.tran 0 10m 5m 2u

.meas AC gain_db FIND 20*log10(mag(V(OUT)/V(IN))) AT=1k

.end
