* High-side current-sense amplifier using the genuine LT6106 macromodel
* 100 mA load current through a 0.1 ohm shunt on a 12 V rail
* Gain = ROUT/RIN = 10k/100 = 100  ->  VOUT = 100 * (100mA * 0.1ohm) = 1.0 V

* Node "IN"  = 12 V rail / high side of the shunt (primary input)
* Node "OUT" = amplifier output across ROUT (primary output)

*** Supply / sensed rail ***
V1 IN 0 12

*** Sense shunt and load ***
RSENSE IN LOAD 0.1
ILOAD  LOAD 0 100m          ; 100 mA load current -> 10 mV sense voltage

*** Gain-setting input resistor (sets the input-to-output transconductance) ***
RIN IN PIN 100              ; RIN = 100 ohm

*** LT6106 current-sense amplifier  (pins: +IN  -IN  OUT  V-) ***
XU1 PIN LOAD OUT 0 LT6106

*** Gain-setting output resistor ***
ROUT OUT 0 10k              ; ROUT = 10k -> gain of 100

*** Analysis ***
.tran 0 2m 0 1u
.meas TRAN vout_dc AVG V(OUT) FROM 1m TO 2m
