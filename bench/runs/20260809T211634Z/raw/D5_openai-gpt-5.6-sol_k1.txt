Precision ADR1399H reference with LTC6078 buffer
VINPUT IN 0 15

* ADR1399H bias and heater connections
RBIAS IN NREF 1k
XREF NREF 0 IN 0 ADR1399H
CREF NREF 0 100n

* Floating 5 V supply keeps the LTC6078 within its supply rating
EVSS VSS 0 IN 0 0.3
EVDD VDD VSS IN 0 0.3333333333
CSUP VDD VSS 100n

* Unity-gain reference buffer
XU1 NREF OUT VDD VSS OUT LTC6078
RLOAD OUT 0 1k
CLOAD OUT 0 100p

.tran 0 20m 0 10u
.meas TRAN vout_dc AVG V(OUT) FROM 15m TO 20m
.options plotwinsize=0

* ADR1399H functional model: 7.05 V reference plus heater
.subckt ADR1399H K A HP HM
VCORE NZ A 7.05
RDYN K NZ 0.5
RHEATER HP HM 1.5k
.ends ADR1399H

* LTC6078 single-channel functional macromodel
* Pin order: noninverting, inverting, positive supply, negative supply, output
.subckt LTC6078 NP NM VP VM O
BAMP NDRV VM V=limit(1e6*V(NP,NM),-0.1,V(VP,VM)+0.1)
ROUT NDRV O 2
RINP NP VM 10T
RINM NM VM 10T
.ends LTC6078

.end
