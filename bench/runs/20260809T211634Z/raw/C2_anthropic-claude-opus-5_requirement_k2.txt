* ADG5248F 8:1 Analog Multiplexer -- Channel 1 to Common Output (D)
* 1 kHz, 500 mV amplitude sine input, +/-15 V supplies, A0=A1=A2=0, EN=high
*
* --- Genuine vendor model (ADI LTspice/PSpice model file) ---
.include ADG5248F.cir
*
* --- Supplies ---
VDD  VDD 0 DC  15
VSS  VSS 0 DC -15
*
* --- Digital control: address 000 selects S1, EN high enables the mux ---
VEN  EN  0 DC 5
VA0  A0  0 DC 0
VA1  A1  0 DC 0
VA2  A2  0 DC 0
*
* --- Signal source into channel 1 ---
VIN  IN  0 SINE(0 0.5 1k)
*
* --- Multiplexer ---
* Pin order: S1 S2 S3 S4 S5 S6 S7 S8 D A0 A1 A2 EN VDD VSS GND
XMUX IN S2 S3 S4 S5 S6 S7 S8 OUT A0 A1 A2 EN VDD VSS 0 ADG5248F
*
* --- Unused source channels terminated to ground (no floating nodes) ---
RS2 S2 0 1k
RS3 S3 0 1k
RS4 S4 0 1k
RS5 S5 0 1k
RS6 S6 0 1k
RS7 S7 0 1k
RS8 S8 0 1k
*
* --- Output load (high impedance so Ron loss is negligible: 0 dB gain) ---
RL  OUT 0 100k
CL  OUT 0 10p
*
* --- Analysis: 5 ms of 1 kHz sine for gain (Vout/Vin) measurement ---
.tran 0 5m 0 1u
.meas TRAN vin_pp  PP V(IN)  FROM=1m TO=5m
.meas TRAN vout_pp PP V(OUT) FROM=1m TO=5m
.meas TRAN gain_db PARAM 20*LOG10(vout_pp/vin_pp)
.options plotwinsize=0
.end
