* LTC6252 High-Speed Amplifier -- Non-Inverting Gain of +2 V/V, +/-5 V Supplies
* Input: 1 V amplitude sine @ 10 MHz on node IN, output on node OUT

.lib LTC6252.lib

*** Supplies ***
VCC VCC 0 DC  5
VEE VEE 0 DC -5

*** Input source: 1 V amplitude, 10 MHz sine (AC=1 for .ac sweep) ***
VIN IN 0 SINE(0 1 10MEG 0 0 0) AC 1

*** Amplifier: non-inverting, gain = 1 + Rf/Rg = 2 ***
* Pin order (LTspice opamp symbol): +IN  -IN  V+  V-  OUT
XU1 IN FB VCC VEE OUT LTC6252

RF OUT FB 1k
RG FB  0  1k

*** Load ***
RL OUT 0 1k
CL OUT 0 2p

*** Analyses ***
.tran 0 1u 0 100p
.ac dec 100 10k 1G

*** Measurements ***
.meas AC gain_db MAX 20*log10(mag(V(OUT)/V(IN)))
.meas TRAN vout_pp PP V(OUT) FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN vin_pp  PP V(IN)  FROM 500n TO 1u
.meas TRAN gain_db_tran PARAM 20*log10(vout_pp/vin_pp)

.options plotwinsize=0 numdgt=7

.end
